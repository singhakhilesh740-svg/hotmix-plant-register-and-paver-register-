Paver Parishisht-1 — Tack coat / spray ke daamar ki nondh

Tack coat wale item ka poora daamar (estimate MT ÷ (density × thickness) × rate) item ke pehle din ek hi baar Plant Parishisht-1 col 7 se aata hai. Phir har din us din bane mix ke area × rate jitna vaparash ghat-ta hai; balance agle din ughdati silak mein.

Paver Parishisht-2 — Tack coat daamar aur mishran ka temperature (truck-wise)

Chainage: Progress ke area ÷ plant ke tonnage = sq.m/T; truck ka net × sq.m/T ÷ (width/2) = lambai. Pehle LHS shuru se aakhir tak, phir RHS shuru se. Temperature site par naapa hua daalo (130°C se kam par laal).

Paver Parishisht-4 — Daamar, kapchi, mishran vaparash (truck-wise)

Pehle upar Parishisht-2 mein Progress ki date confirm karo.

Samay = plant + lead time. Area = truck ki lambai × lane width. Sarasari moti: L/C/R daaloge to unka average; khali ho to mishran ÷ (area × density) se nikla (calc). Niyat moti se kam par laal.

🧪 Test schedule — MoRTH Section 900 (minimum frequency)

⚠ Frequency MoRTH 5th revision (Table 900-3/900-4) ke hisaab se bhari hai — apne tender / QAP se ek baar milao; farak ho to neeche "Test list" mein badal do.
